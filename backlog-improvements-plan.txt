H. Capacity booking — multi-trip request at pickup (SHIPPED)
======================================================================
Status: shipped (implement Nov 2026).

Problem
- Cargo may need **more than one vehicle load** to finish a job.
- Office and driver must agree on **how many round trips** before the job continues and
  the customer is charged accordingly.

Goal (customer)
- After the driver **has arrived at the pickup location**, the customer can request
  **multiple round trips** to complete the booking.
- Customer enters an **estimated number of round trips**.
- The booking does **not** proceed on the new trip count until **office approves** and
  the **driver accepts**.

Goal (office / driver)
- Staff or System Admin **reviews** the request (expect accept in normal ops for revenue).
- On **reject**, office cancels the booking; customer creates a **new booking** if needed.
- After office **accepts**, the **driver must accept** before the trip continues under
  the updated terms.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
Locked decisions
----------------------------------------------------------------------
| Topic | Decision |
| When customer can request multi-trip | Only **after driver marks arrived at pickup** (not at booking create) |
| Driver before “arrived” | Booking must already be **in_transit**; driver then records **Arrived at pickup** (new step after in_transit) |
| Multi-trip submissions | **One submit only** per booking — no customer edits/resubmit; office approve or reject only |
| Who approves (office) | **Staff OR System Admin** — one office approval |
| Office trip count | Office may set final round trips on approve; if **different from customer estimate**, **customer must confirm** before driver accept |
| After customer confirm (if needed) | **Driver must accept** updated trip count before proceeding |
| Billable unit | **One round trip** = pickup → dropoff → **return to pickup** |
| Customer input | Estimated **round trip count** (integer ≥ 1), single submission |
| Invoice before approval | **Original invoice unchanged** (see create rule below) |
| Invoice after approval | **Replace** amount with `approved_round_trips × list fare` for the booked pricing type (update snapshot on booking/invoice) |
| At booking create (supersedes shipped F) | **Revert auto multi-trip**: invoice = **1 × list fare** at create regardless of cargo ÷ capacity |
| Office reject | **Cancel booking**; customer must **submit a new booking** if they still need service |
| Change vehicle type | Any time **after arrived at pickup**; **once per active booking**; **wrong-truck fee** = **% of list fare** for booked type — rate in **Settings** (Admin-configurable key; seed default at implement) |

----------------------------------------------------------------------
Workflow (target lifecycle)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Customer creates booking → invoice **1 × fare** (pending → gatepass → driver accept → **in_transit**).
2. Driver sets **in_transit**, then records **Arrived at pickup** (new).
3. Customer (active booking) → **Request additional round trips** ( **one submission** ) + estimated count.
   - Booking enters **pending multi-trip approval** (name/flags at implement).
4. Staff/Admin → **Approve** (final round trip count) **or Reject** → reject **cancels booking**.
5. If office final count **≠ customer estimate** → customer **must confirm** revised count.
6. Driver **Accept** updated trip count (acceptance gate after office approve + any customer confirm).
7. Job continues to completion with **updated invoice** (`approved_round_trips × fare`).
8. Any time after step 2: customer may **change vehicle type once** → Settings-based **% of list fare** fee on invoice.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
UI / portal surfaces (when building)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Customer
- After **arrived at pickup**: **Request more round trips** (single submit; no edit after send).
- If office changes trip count: **Confirm revised count** before driver accept.
- After **arrived at pickup**: **Change truck type** (once per booking) + fee warning (% of list fare from Settings).
- Reject outcome: cancelled booking; prompt to create new booking.

Driver
- **in_transit** as today, then **Arrived at pickup** (geofence optional post-MVP).
- **Accept updated trip count** after office approve (+ customer confirm if count changed).

Staff / Admin
- Queue or booking detail: **Multi-trip requests** — approve (with final trip count) or reject (cancel).
- Same permissions for Staff and Admin on this workflow unless permissions_matrix says otherwise.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
Pricing & invoice (when building)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- **Create**: `invoice_amount = 1 × pricing.amount` (no `ceil(cargo ÷ capacity)` multiplier).
- **On office approve**: `invoice_amount = approved_round_trips × pricing.amount` (recalculate snapshot).
- **Truck change fee**: `pricing.amount × (setting_percent / 100)` added to invoice (Setting key; Admin UI).
- Align with `CreateBookingInvoice`, booking payout snapshots, and payment proof flows.
