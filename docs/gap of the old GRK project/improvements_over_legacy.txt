What the new GRK Trucking Services 2.0 improves over the old system

The new project is a fuller, clearer version of the old truck booking and tracking site. Instead of three kinds of users, there are four: customers, drivers, office staff, and a system administrator with full control. Sign-in is safer (verified email and protected passwords), and removed records can be archived instead of wiped forever. The delivery flow stays familiar—request a truck, office uploads the gate pass, a driver accepts, the trip moves, then paperwork and proof of delivery finish the job—but prices are managed in a price list the admin can change, fares come from that pricing list (no unpaid/paid invoice in MVP), and after a driver accepts they can see a map route to the destination (not only a status label). Drivers only see jobs that match their truck type and can handle one active delivery at a time; at the end they can add photos and a signature, and customers can rate the whole trip. The office gets clearer permissions for staff versus admin, a history of important actions, better earnings summaries, and email updates when a booking’s status changes. Overall it keeps the heart of the old school project and adds the practical pieces that made the old app feel incomplete compared with modern delivery services.
