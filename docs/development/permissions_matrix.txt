Settings                            No         No         No                 Yes

Replace gatepass                    No         No         Yes                Yes
Archive / soft-delete booking       No         No         No                 Yes

Earnings & reports                  No         No         No                 Yes
Activity logs                       No         No         No                 Yes
Rate overall trip                   Own done   No         No                 View
