GK Trucking Services 2.0 — Booking lifecycle

Statuses
pending | accepted | in_transit | completed | cancelled

Normal path
1. Customer or Admin creates booking → pending
   - Payout copied from Pricing for vehicle_type
   - Invoice may be created unpaid
2. Staff or Admin uploads gatepass (first time)
   - Still pending; now visible to matching drivers
   - Email matching drivers (when email phase is built)
3. Driver accepts → accepted
   - is_locked = true; driver_id + vehicle_id set
   - Vehicle status → in_use
   - Max one active delivery per driver (accepted or in_transit)
4. Driver → in_transit
5. Driver uploads EIR; on complete also POD (photos)
6. Driver → completed only if EIR AND POD both exist
   - Vehicle → available (if it was in_use)

Cancel
- Staff: may cancel only BEFORE gatepass is uploaded
- Admin: may cancel anytime (including after gatepass)
- On cancel with assigned vehicle: release vehicle to available; unlock as needed

After gatepass is uploaded
- Staff cannot change status (including cancel)
- Staff cannot replace gatepass
- Admin can change status and replace gatepass

Admin
- Full CRUD on bookings and related docs
- Status overrides must keep vehicle lock/state consistent

Dropped
- Driver clearance workflow (not in product)

Map
- Static Mapbox route on details only after accept (pickup/dropoff lat-lng)
